# Solar Bill Check (working name)

Snap your electricity bill and find out, in about 30 seconds, whether rooftop solar is worth it for your home. The app shows the right system size, the cost after the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, when it pays back, and how to apply.

Built for [Environmental Hacks](https://www.wemakedevs.org/aws/env) (WeMakeDevs × AWS Builder Center, Oct 8–11 2026), Waste and Energy track.

Status: in progress.
