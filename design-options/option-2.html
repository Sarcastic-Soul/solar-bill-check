Common questions

I live in a flat. Can I still get the subsidy?

Yes, through your housing society (RWA). It gets ₹18,000 per kW for a shared system.

My bill is zero because of free units.

Then solar may not save you money right now. We'll tell you honestly.

Do you apply for me?

No. The official portal needs your Aadhaar and OTP, so only you should apply. We fill in a sheet you can copy from.

Before you apply

  • Bill is in the residential category
  • Sanctioned load (3 kW) is enough
  • Name on bill must match your bank account
    Bill is in your father's name? Get it changed at the DISCOM office first, or the subsidy can't be paid.
